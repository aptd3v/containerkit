---
title: "Capabilities"
---

[CapAdd](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/aptd3v/containerkit/pkg/kit#CapAdd) and [CapDrop](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/aptd3v/containerkit/pkg/kit#CapDrop) take a [Capability](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/aptd3v/containerkit/pkg/kit#Capability). `ALL` is re-exported. The rest of the kernel list lives in `pkg/cap`, under the names Docker accepts (`NET_BIND_SERVICE`, not `CAP_NET_BIND_SERVICE`).

```go
import (
    . "github.com/aptd3v/containerkit/pkg/kit"
    "github.com/aptd3v/containerkit/pkg/cap"
)

svc := NewService(
    Name("api"),
    Image("alpine:latest"),
    CapAdd(cap.NET_BIND_SERVICE),
    CapDrop(ALL),
    Privileged(false),
    ReadOnly(true),
    Tty(false),
)
```

[Privileged](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/aptd3v/containerkit/pkg/kit#Privileged) on the service is the container flag. Inside [Build](/build-and-dockerfile) it is `build.privileged`. [ReadOnly](/mount-a-volume) here is the root filesystem. [Tty](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/aptd3v/containerkit/pkg/kit#Tty) allocates a TTY. [Interactive](https://pkg.go.dev/github.com/aptd3v/containerkit/pkg/kit#Interactive) keeps stdin open.
